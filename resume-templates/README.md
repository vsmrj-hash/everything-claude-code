## Region-by-Region Guide

### 🇺🇸 USA — Resume
- **Length:** 1 page (2 for 10+ years experience)
- **Photo:** Never include
- **Key sections:** Summary, Core Competencies, Experience, Education, Certifications
- **ATS tips:** Use standard section headers, avoid tables/headers/footers for critical info, include keywords verbatim from job descriptions
- **Tone:** Achievement-focused, quantified metrics in every bullet

### 🇬🇧 UK — CV
- **Length:** 2 pages standard
- **Photo:** Not expected (to avoid discrimination claims)
- **Key sections:** Personal Profile, Career History, Education, Key Skills, References
- **Style:** Concise British English; avoid American spellings; use "CV" not "resume"
- **Note:** Include month/year for all dates; UK employers value chronological clarity

### 🇪🇺 EU / Europass
- **Length:** 2–3 pages
- **Photo:** Optional but common in many EU countries (Germany, France, Austria)
- **Key sections:** Personal info, Work Experience, Education, Language Skills (CEFR), Digital Skills
- **GDPR note:** Do not include sensitive personal data beyond what's necessary
- **EU AI Act compliance:** Data Scientists should note regulatory alignment experience

### 🇯🇵 Japan — 履歴書 (Rirekisho)
- **Length:** Structured 1-page grid (A3 folded to A4, or digital equivalent)
- **Photo:** **Required** — formal passport-style, 4cm × 3cm, top-right
- **Language:** Japanese primary; English acceptable for global companies
- **Key sections:** Personal info table, Education/Career history (chronological), Certifications, Motivation/Self-PR
- **Cultural notes:** Handwritten versions still respected; use keigo (polite language) in Japanese sections; include emergency contact

### 🇮🇳 India — Resume
- **Length:** 2–3 pages acceptable
- **Photo:** Common to include (top-right corner)
- **Key sections:** Career Objective, Personal Details (DOB, father's name, marital status), Work Experience, Education, Declaration
- **Salary info:** Include Current CTC and Expected CTC — actively expected by Indian recruiters
- **Notice period:** Always state; 30/60/90 days is standard in Indian job market

### 🇦🇺 Australia — Resume
- **Length:** 2–3 pages
- **Photo:** Not included (anti-discrimination)
- **Key sections:** Professional Summary, Career History, Education, Licences & Certifications, Key Skills, Referees
- **Important:** Include Working Rights status; list relevant licences (White Card, etc.)
- **Referees:** "Available on request" is standard; do not include referee contact details upfront

### 🇳🇿 New Zealand — CV
- **Length:** 2–3 pages
- **Photo:** Not included
- **Key sections:** Professional Profile, Registration/Work Rights, Employment History, Education, Clinical/Technical Skills, Referees
- **Important:** State NZ work rights clearly (citizen/resident/visa type)
- **Cultural note:** Acknowledge Te Tiriti o Waitangi awareness where relevant (health, education, public sector roles)

## How to Customise

1. Open any `.docx` file in Microsoft Word, Google Docs, or LibreOffice
2. Replace all placeholder text (names, companies, dates, figures) with your own information
3. Keep the formatting intact — colour scheme, fonts, and spacing are calibrated for professional impact
4. For ATS submissions (USA/UK), save as `.docx` — not PDF — unless the job ad specifies PDF
